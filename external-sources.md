# External Sources Used

The destination explorer links directly to official immigration portals, including Canada (`https://www.canada.ca/en/services/immigration-citizenship.html`), the United Kingdom (`https://www.gov.uk/browse/visas-immigration`), Australia (`https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing`), the United States (`https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas.html`), New Zealand (`https://www.immigration.govt.nz/new-zealand-visas/`), France (`https://france-visas.gouv.fr/en/`), Italy (`https://vistoperitalia.esteri.it/home/en`), Spain (`https://www.exteriores.gob.es/en/ServiciosAlCiudadano/Paginas/Visados.aspx`), Ireland (`https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-visit-ireland/`), Türkiye (`https://www.evisa.gov.tr/en/`), the UAE (`https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id`), Malaysia (`https://www.imi.gov.my/index.php/en/`), Hungary (`https://enterhungary.gov.hu/eh/`), Romania (`https://evisa.mae.ro/`), and Cyprus (`https://www.gov.cy/mfa/en/documents/visa-information/`).

Official processing-time references reviewed during content preparation were Canada IRCC (`https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html`), UK Visas and Immigration (`https://www.gov.uk/guidance/visa-processing-times-applications-outside-the-uk`), Australian Home Affairs (`https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-processing-times/global-visa-processing-times`), and Immigration New Zealand (`https://www.immigration.govt.nz/process-to-apply/waiting-for-a-visa/processing-a-visa-application/how-long-it-takes-to-process-an-application/check-visa-application-processing-time/`).

The cinematic globe uses the NASA Scientific Visualization Studio image titled **“Equirectangular Projected Earth for LARGEST”**, retrieved through image search and uploaded to the project’s managed asset storage as `/manus-storage/d2d-earth-texture_492eb1a2.jpg`.

The About section uses an Unsplash airport terminal sunset image retrieved through image search and uploaded as `/manus-storage/d2d-airport-sunset_46f94748.jpg`.
