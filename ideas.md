# Dreams To Destination — Experience Direction

The website will use a **midnight-atlas** visual language: deep navy surfaces, warm ivory content zones, electric blue interaction cues, and restrained champagne-gold details. The result should feel more like a premium international advisory firm than a conventional visa agency. Space Grotesk carries the display voice while Inter supports dense, accessible reading.

| Layer | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | Asymmetric editorial composition with a luminous WebGL-inspired globe, orbital route lines, a floating passport card, concise conversion copy, and immediate consultation access. |
| Surfaces | Soft ivory for high-reading sections, near-black navy for immersive sections, translucent blue glass for interactive panels, and thin gold highlights rather than heavy gold fills. |
| Motion | Scroll reveals, restrained 3D card tilt, ambient glows, orbital motion, progress transitions, and hover depth. Motion remains optional when reduced motion is preferred. |
| Conversion | A persistent navigation CTA, repeated contextual CTAs, short forms, visible progress, reassuring privacy language, and clear success/error feedback. |
| Trust | Transparent process language, user-supplied performance indicators, official immigration portal links, qualified eligibility wording, and no invented testimonials or staff biographies. |

The content hierarchy will move visitors from aspiration to clarity: hero promise, service fit, destination discovery, self-assessment, company credibility, process transparency, consultation booking, frequently asked questions, and direct contact. The primary action is always **Book a consultation**; the secondary action is **Check eligibility**.

The destination explorer uses broad planning windows rather than guaranteed processing times. Each destination links to its official government immigration portal, and a visible disclaimer explains that timing and document requirements depend on visa stream, nationality, application location, and case complexity.

The success-stories component will support verified client content but will not ship with fictional reviews, names, photos, outcomes, or ratings. Until the business supplies authentic permissioned stories, the interface will show a transparent invitation to request references during a consultation.
